🔐 Authentication Required

Most endpoints require JWT authentication. To get started:

  • Step 1: Use POST /api/v1/auth/register to create an account
  • Step 2: Use POST /api/v1/auth/login to get your JWT token
  • Step 3: Click the "Authorize" 🔓 button below and enter: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN
  • Step 4: Now you can test all authenticated endpoints!
